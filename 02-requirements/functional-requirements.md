## 1. Conventions

- **ID format:** `FR-###` (sequential, never reused).
- **Priority:** MoSCoW — Must / Should / Could / Won't (this time).
- **Status:** Draft → Reviewed → Approved → In Development → Delivered → Verified.
- Every requirement must be atomic (one testable statement), unambiguous, and traceable to a business objective.
