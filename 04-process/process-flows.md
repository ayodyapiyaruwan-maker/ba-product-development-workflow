## Conventions

- **ID format:** `PF-###`.
- Prefer diagrams (BPMN, swimlane, flowchart) stored alongside this file (e.g., `PF-001-diagram.png`/`.drawio`/`.mmd`) with this document providing the written walkthrough, decision points, and business rules referenced.
- Distinguish clearly between **current-state ("as-is")** and **future-state ("to-be")** flows — never blend them in one diagram/description.
