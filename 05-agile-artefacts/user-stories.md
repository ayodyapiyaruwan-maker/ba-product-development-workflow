## Conventions

- **ID format:** `US-###`.
- **Format:** As a [persona], I want [capability], so that [benefit].
- Each story should be small enough to deliver within a single sprint/iteration; split larger stories (epics) explicitly.
- Every story must trace to at least one functional requirement and one persona.
